The rules

Do and don't

Do

  • Make it shoppable and link the exact product you are talking about
  • Hold the bottle. Label to the lens, in your hand, not a graphic
  • Say one claim that is printed on the label, then move on
  • Say results vary, out loud, in every video
  • Space posts 2 to 3 days apart, one a day maximum
  • Tell us when something flops — that is data, not a failure

Don't

  • Say it treats, cures, heals, reverses or prevents anything
  • Name a disease or condition, yours or anyone else's
  • Name another brand, or describe what they put in their bottles
  • Promise a timeline — no "in three days", no before-and-afters without results vary
  • Batch four videos into one afternoon. They cannibalise each other
  • Repost the same video to hit a count

Samples

  • Request through TikTok Shop, not DMs, so it is tracked against your account.
  • A sample is for content. If nothing goes up within two weeks we stop approving them.
  • Tell us which flavour you want — we will not guess.
